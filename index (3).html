Emil Social Studio

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Development status

This application is currently under development. In the current development version, Instagram and YouTube account connection, account-data retrieval and publishing are not active. The app does not currently collect social-platform login credentials or access tokens. This policy describes the current position and the data handling planned for integrations if and when they are enabled. The policy will be reviewed before those integrations are released.

1. Who is responsible

Emil Social Studio is developed and operated by Emil, an individual developer based in Greece. For privacy questions, data-access requests or deletion requests, contact anghelemil@gmail.com.

2. Information the app may handle

The current local development version may store information that the user enters or imports, such as account labels, social-media handles, photographs, videos, draft captions, hashtags, content plans and scheduling preferences. This information is used to provide the app's local content-management features.

If official platform integrations are enabled in a future release, the app may also process, as needed for the features the user chooses to use:

  • Platform account identifiers, usernames and basic profile information made available through the platform's authorization flow.
  • Authorization tokens required to connect the user's own account. Tokens will be handled by the app for authentication and API requests and will not be requested as plain-text passwords.
  • Media, captions, hashtags, planned publication times and publication status/results that the user chooses to prepare, schedule or publish.
  • Limited technical information returned by the platform API, such as media identifiers and error/status responses.

The app is intended to use official platform APIs and authorization flows. It is not intended to collect or store a user's Instagram or Google password.

3. How information is used

Information is used to organize the user's own social-media accounts and media, prepare drafts, manage a content calendar, and—only after the user has reviewed and explicitly approved an item—request publication through a connected platform. The app is designed to require user approval before publication; it is not intended to publish unapproved drafts.

4. Storage and sharing

The current app is designed as a local desktop application. Information entered into the current development version is stored on the user's device in the app's local project/database folders. The developer does not currently operate a central service that receives the user's drafts or media.

If a platform integration is enabled, the app will send only the API requests and information needed to perform the action selected by the user to the relevant platform, such as Meta/Instagram or Google/YouTube. Those providers process information under their own privacy policies and terms. The privacy policy and app behavior will be updated if additional services or remote storage are introduced.

5. Publication and user control

Connecting an account does not by itself mean that content will be published. The intended workflow is draft, review, explicit approval and then publication or scheduling. Users remain responsible for checking the selected account, media, caption, hashtags and publication time before approving content.

6. Retention and deletion

In the current local version, users can remove locally stored drafts, media and account records using the app or by deleting the relevant local app data. Keep a separate backup before deleting project folders if any files may be needed later.

When platform connections become available, users will be able to disconnect or revoke the app's access through the relevant platform settings. Users may also email anghelemil@gmail.com to request deletion of personal data handled by the app. A request will be reviewed and handled as required by applicable law. Information that must be retained by law, or that remains with a platform under that platform's own policies, may not be removable by the app operator.

7. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures will be used to protect information handled by the app. Because the current version is under development, users should keep their computer and backups secure and should not share local project folders containing private media or account data. The app will not be released with account authorization enabled until its token-storage and deletion behavior has been reviewed.

8. Legal rights

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, including the GDPR, individuals in the European Economic Area may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal data, and to object to certain processing. They may also have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data-protection supervisory authority. To exercise a right, contact the email above. Additional information may be requested to verify the identity of the requester and protect account data.

9. Children

Emil Social Studio is a productivity tool for people managing their own social-media content. It is not designed for children and is not knowingly intended to collect personal data directly from children.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app develops or its data practices change. The latest version will be published at this page with an updated revision date. Material changes will be reflected before the relevant new functionality is made available.

11. Contact

Privacy and data requests: anghelemil@gmail.com.
